# Widamine Color Palette

This palette follows a **60–30–10 design rule**, where one dominant color is supported by a secondary color and a small accent color. It also includes neutral colors for backgrounds and typography.

---

## 1. Blue Ball (Primary Color)

**Hex:** `#009FD6`
**RGB:** `0, 159, 214`
**Usage:** **60%** of the interface

### Characteristics
- Bright cyan-blue
- Clean and energetic
- Modern and trustworthy
- Feels medical, tech, and professional

### Best Uses
- Hero sections
- Primary buttons
- Icons
- Links
- Navigation highlights
- Main branding color
- Illustrations

### Emotional Tone
- Trust, Freshness, Innovation, Reliability, Cleanliness

---

## 2. Yellow Sea (Secondary Color)

**Hex:** `#F7A269`
**RGB:** `247, 162, 105`
**Usage:** **30%** of the interface

### Characteristics
- Warm peach-orange
- Soft rather than aggressive
- Friendly and welcoming
- Adds warmth to the cool blue

### Best Uses
- Secondary buttons
- Highlights
- Cards
- Section backgrounds
- Hover states
- Decorative elements

### Emotional Tone
- Warmth, Comfort, Optimism, Hospitality, Human touch

---

## 3. Black Rose (Accent Color)

**Hex:** `#6D0024`
**RGB:** `109, 0, 36`
**Usage:** **10%** of the interface

### Characteristics
- Deep burgundy
- Rich wine red
- Luxurious
- Elegant
- Strong visual contrast

### Best Uses
- Small accents
- Premium badges
- Important icons
- Decorative lines
- Luxury branding touches
- CTA emphasis (sparingly)

### Emotional Tone
- Luxury, Sophistication, Confidence, Exclusivity, Elegance

---

## 4. Attractive White (Neutral Background)

**Hex:** `#FBF7EF`
**RGB:** `251, 247, 239`

### Characteristics
- Warm off-white
- Slight cream tint
- Much softer than pure white
- Makes colorful elements stand out without harsh contrast

### Best Uses
- Main page background
- Large content sections
- Cards
- Forms
- Empty space

### Emotional Tone
- Calm, Airy, Premium, Comfortable, Minimalist

---

## 5. Blackout (Dark Neutral)

**Hex:** `#1E1E1E`
**RGB:** `30, 30, 30`

### Characteristics
- Near-black charcoal
- Easier on the eyes than pure black
- Excellent readability
- Modern and understated

### Best Uses
- Body text
- Headings
- Icons
- Footer background
- Dark sections
- Navigation text

### Emotional Tone
- Stability, Professionalism, Strength, Modern elegance

---

## Overall Palette Analysis

| Role                | Color                      | Purpose                                              |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary (60%)**   | `#009FD6` Blue Ball        | Branding, key actions, dominant visual identity      |
| **Secondary (30%)** | `#F7A269` Yellow Sea       | Warmth, supporting UI elements, highlights           |
| **Accent (10%)**    | `#6D0024` Black Rose       | Luxury accents, emphasis, premium details            |
| **Background**      | `#FBF7EF` Attractive White | Soft, inviting canvas for content                    |
| **Text / Dark UI**  | `#1E1E1E` Blackout         | High readability, strong contrast, modern typography |

### Design Personality
- **Professional** through the vibrant blue.
- **Friendly and approachable** through the warm peach.
- **Premium and elegant** through the deep burgundy.
- **Clean and spacious** thanks to the warm off-white.
- **Modern and readable** with the charcoal black.
